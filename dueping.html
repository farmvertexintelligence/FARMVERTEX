questions.

Where does the customer's payment go?

Directly to your own UPI ID. DuePing only shows your UPI button and QR code. It never receives or holds your money.

Will my customers feel offended?

The first reminders are friendly notes before the due date. Messages become firmer only if a bill stays unpaid for weeks, and you can mark a bill paid at any time to stop reminders.

I already use Khatabook or Vyapar. Do I need this?

Keep them for your accounts. DuePing does one job: it keeps reminding customers automatically on a schedule until they pay, without you pressing anything.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is prepaid monthly and doesn't auto-renew. If you don't renew, you move to the free plan and your data stays safe.
