Common questions.

Who owns the code?

You do, once the project is paid for, as set out in our written agreement. Your software, your control.

How do you price projects?

Most projects are fixed-price against a written scope. Ongoing work is billed monthly. Every quote is itemised so you can see what you are paying for.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in India and are open to international engagements.

What if I already have software?

We can maintain, extend or integrate with it. We will review it first and tell you honestly whether to improve it or replace it.

Tell us what you want to build.

Share the problem, the process or the product idea. We will reply with a clear next step and an honest view of what it takes.
