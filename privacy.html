Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. Effective 29 September 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by FarmVertex Intelligence, a proprietorship enterprise in Tamil Nadu, India (GSTIN 33QKOPK2592B1ZU), owned by Vaideeswaran R K. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean FarmVertex Intelligence.

What we collect

  • Details you send us. When you use the contact form, email or call us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation and message.
  • Technical data. Our website host may record standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Analytics, only with your consent. If we enable website analytics, it runs only after you accept it in the cookie banner. It tells us how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To provide, invoice and support services you engage us for.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To understand and improve the website, if you allow analytics.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our email, hosting and analytics providers, and only as needed for the purposes above. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. Some providers may process data outside India.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies by default. If analytics is enabled, analytics cookies are set only after you accept them, and you can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting work, and business records for as long as Indian law requires. We then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent you have given. Email hello@farmvertex.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
